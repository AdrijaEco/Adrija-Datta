About me

From agricultural landscapes to ecological networks.

I study plant–pollinator interactions, beneficial insects, and how climate and land use shape biodiversity and ecosystem services.

I am a Ph.D. scholar in Earth Sciences at IIT Gandhinagar, with a submitted thesis. My training in horticulture and agricultural meteorology informs my work across ecological modelling, climate data, and food systems. In 2026, I spent four months at UFZ Leipzig with Dr. Rohini Kumar.

Questions I work on.

Biodiversity & food
